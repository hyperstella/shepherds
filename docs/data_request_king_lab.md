# Draft: request to use the King/Strömbom sheep–dog GPS data

**To:** the contact listed on https://www.shoalgroup.org/data (Andrew King's group, Swansea)
**Subject:** Using your sheep–dog GPS data (King et al. 2012; Strömbom et al. 2014) in a research project

---

Dear Dr King,

I am a student at Columbia University working on a research project about learning the interaction
rules of herded sheep flocks from trajectory data. So far we have reanalysed the UWB tracking data of
Jadhav et al. (2024, Communications Biology) for 14 merino sheep and a border collie. We fit
nonparametric sheep–sheep and sheep–dog interaction kernels and compare them with a calibrated
Strömbom-type shepherding model.

We would like to test whether our results hold for a different flock and dog, and your GPS data of
46 merino sheep and a kelpie (King et al. 2012, Current Biology; Strömbom et al. 2014, J. R. Soc.
Interface) would be ideal for that. Before downloading it from the link on your data page, could
you let us know:

1. whether you are happy for us to use the data for this project, and how you would like it cited
   (we would cite both papers);
2. whether we may publish trajectories or summary statistics derived from it, for example in a paper
   and in an open-source repository, or whether we should share only code and results;
3. whether there is metadata on the three herding events (durations, which periods the dog was
   driving rather than collecting, sampling rate, and any known tracking gaps).

We are happy to share our analysis code and results with you.
